# Estimator 10-Minute Practical: "Approach and Tools" (CANDIDATE-FACING)

> **Interviewer setup:** print or show this file only. The answer key is in `answer-keys/tests/estimator-10-minute-practical-KEY.md`. Give the candidate paper and a basic calculator. A laptop with the company's takeoff tool is optional; this test is about **approach**, not a finished number.

## Timing (10 minutes total)
| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0:00–2:00 | Read the scenario silently. |
| 2:00–9:00 | Talk through your approach **or** write bullet points. The interviewer only listens and takes notes. |
| 9:00–10:00 | Give your list of the **top 3 questions or RFIs** you'd send before pricing. |

**Instructions:**
- Explain **how** you'd estimate this job: the steps, which **tools and software** you'd use at each step, what you'd check, and what you'd include or exclude.
- You don't need a final price.
- If you use rough math, say where the rates would come from.
- It's fine to say "I'd need to ask about…"

## Scenario: Retail store refresh, "Building 2" (drawings described in text)
**Bid documents received** (PDF set, 14 sheets, plus a spec section excerpt):
- **A-101 Floor Plan** (scale shown as 1/8" = 1'-0"; sheet is marked "Reduced, print at 50%"). Building footprint 150' × 80'.
  - **Sales Floor:** about 9,000 SF.
  - **Back of House** (stockroom, receiving): about 2,400 SF.
  - **Restrooms and office:** about 600 SF, getting new LVT by others.
- **A-601 Finish Schedule:**
  - Sales Floor: "PC-1, polished concrete."
  - Back of House: "SC-1, grind and seal."
  - Restrooms: "LVT by others."
- **Spec 03 35 43 excerpt:**
  - "PC-1: Aggregate exposure Class B. Appearance Level 2 (satin). Apply lithium-silicate densifier and stain guard. Fill control joints with semi-rigid polyurea. Contractor to verify existing conditions."
  - "SC-1: Grind to remove contaminants; apply penetrating sealer per manufacturer."
- **Existing conditions note (A-001):**
  - "Existing slab approx. 6 years old. Previous tenant had VCT in front 1/3 of sales floor; flooring removed by demo contractor, mastic may remain."
  - "Control joints approx. 15' o.c. each way."
  - "Store remains closed during work."
- **C-101 Site Plan (parking restripe):**
  - 1 parking lot, about 72 stalls total, including 4 accessible stalls, 1 of them van-accessible.
  - 2 crosswalks.
  - 1 fire lane along the building front, about 300 LF of curb, marked "FIRE LANE – NO PARKING" with red curb paint.
  - Directional arrows (6).
  - Note: "Restripe to match existing layout. Remove conflicting markings."
- **General:**
  - Work hours 7am–5pm Mon–Fri.
  - Bid due in 5 days.
  - GC requires a schedule duration with the bid.
  - Site is 2 hours from the shop.

### Tell us:
You won't cover everything. Prioritize what would most change the price or the risk.

1. **Approach and tools:** your step-by-step approach from opening the documents to submitting, and the tools and software you'd use at each step, including any AI or automation and how you'd check it.
2. **Checks and risks:** what you'd check or verify, and what could go wrong.
3. **Labor and material sources:** how you'd come up with labor production, material, and equipment, and where those numbers would come from.
4. **Top 3 RFIs:** your top 3 RFIs or questions, plus the key inclusions, exclusions, and clarifications you'd put in the proposal.

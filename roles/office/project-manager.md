## A. PM core
| ID | Type | Question |
|---|---|---|
| PM-A1 ★ | B | "Walk me through how you took a job from award to closeout on your last project." |
| PM-A2 ★ | B | "Tell me about a change order you had to fight for. How did you document it?" |
| PM-A3 | S | "The GC's schedule moves your polish start up 2 weeks, and the slab isn't fully cured and other trades will still be on it. What do you do?" |
| PM-A4 ★ | B | "How do you track whether a job is making money while it's running?" |
| PM-A5 | B | "Tell me about a time a customer or GC was unhappy with the finished floor or striping. What happened?" |
| PM-A6 | S | "Your crew reports silica dust complaints from another trade on site. What do you do today?" |
| PM-A7 (senior) | B | "How have you managed several jobs and crews at once? How did you decide priorities?" |

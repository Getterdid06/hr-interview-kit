# Field Tools and Tech Module (add to any field role)

Answer key: `answer-keys/field/field-tools-tech-module-KEY.md`
Why it's here: field-to-office communication and data entry are among contractors' top technology pain points (AGC/Sage 2025 outlook). Field roles should be able to capture time, photos, and daily info correctly.

## Position tuning
```yaml
apps_we_use: ["?"]   # list the actual apps: e.g., Busybusy/ClockShark/ExakTime (time), CompanyCam (photos), Raken/Procore (daily logs), Bluebeam/Procore Drawings (plans)
expected_level: basic   # basic (helper) | proficient (operator/tech) | advanced (foreman)
```

| ID | Type | Question |
|---|---|---|
| FT-1 ★ | B | "What app have you used to clock in and out? What do you do if you forget or the app won't work?" |
| FT-2 | B | "Have you used plans or drawings on a tablet or phone? How do you know you're on the latest sheet?" |
| FT-3 ★ | S | "You finish an area. What photos would you take, and why?" |
| FT-4 | B | "Have you filled out a daily report, JHA, or inspection checklist on a phone? What goes in it?" |
| FT-5 | S | "You find damage on the floor before you start (crack, stain, gouge). What do you do?" |
| FT-6 | B | "Has an app ever made your day harder, like double entry, no signal, or too many notifications? What did you do?" |

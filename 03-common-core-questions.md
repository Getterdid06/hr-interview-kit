# 03 — Common Core: Data Management, AI Tools, and Tool Impact

Ask 3–5 of these of every candidate. Pick the **Office (O)** or **Field (F)** variant. ★ = must-ask. Answer key: `answer-keys/03-common-core-KEY.md`.

Context: AGC/Sage's 2026 outlook found 61% of contractors use AI or plan to invest more in it, mostly for office/admin (45%) and estimating (23%) work. The 2025 survey listed the biggest tech pain points as cybersecurity, time to implement and train, employee resistance, field-office communication, and software integration. These questions test whether a candidate gets the benefit of tools without the pain.

| ID | Type | Question (O = office variant, F = field variant) | Probes |
|---|---|---|---|
| CORE-1 ★ | B | **O:** "Walk me through the software you used in a normal week at your last job: what each one was for and where the data went next." **F:** "What apps or devices did you use on the job: time clock, photos, daily reports, plans on a tablet, machine apps?" | Which was the "source of truth"? Who else used that data? |
| CORE-2 ★ | B | "Tell me about a time a tool or app made your job **faster or more accurate**. What changed, and by how much?" | Before vs. after? How did you know it was better? |
| CORE-3 ★ | B | "Tell me about a time a tool or software made your job **more complicated** or caused a mistake. What happened and what did you do?" | Was it training, bad data, double entry, or the tool itself? |
| CORE-4 | S | **O:** "An AI tool gives you a takeoff, summary, or number that looks off. What do you do before you use it?" **F:** "The app or plan on the tablet shows something different from what the foreman told you. What do you do?" | How do you check it? Who do you tell? |
| CORE-5 ★ (O) | S | "What information would you **never** put into a public AI tool like a free chatbot?" | Client drawings under NDA, bid numbers, payroll, bank info, personal info? |
| CORE-6 | B | **O:** "How do you name, file, and version documents so someone else could find the current one without asking you?" **F:** "How do you make sure your photos and daily notes are useful to the office later?" | Example of a naming convention. Superseded drawings. |
| CORE-7 | B | "How do you learn a new piece of software or a new machine feature? Give a recent example." | How long did it take? Did you teach anyone else? |
| CORE-8 | S | "If you could fix one thing about how data moves between the field and the office, what would it be?" | Practical, or blaming? |
| CORE-9 (O) | B | "Tell me about a time you found bad or duplicate data, such as wrong cost codes, a duplicate vendor, or an old price. How did you find it and fix it?" | Root cause fixed, or one-off? |
| CORE-10 | B | "What AI tools, if any, have you used for work? What did you use them for, and where do you *not* trust them?" | Specific product names. A real limitation they've seen. |
